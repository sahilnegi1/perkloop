Broken-heart handout (die-cut, not a rectangle)

Curiosity outside, loyalty message + QR inside. Separate from the transaction flow — this is promotion only.
Print specs (cheap & easy)
250–300gsm art paper, color, ~90mm wide heart. Cut on the SOLID outline. Score/fold or micro-perf on the DASHED zigzag. Stick the outlet's static QR in the box before printing the batch.
Distribution
Hand out near cafes/salons with permission, bill folders, college gates. Outside: “Dil tootna normal hai…” Inside: “…reward milna special hai! Every visit counts — Perk Loop.” Never claim a partnership that doesn't exist.
