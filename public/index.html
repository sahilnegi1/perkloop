Frequently asked questions

Can I use Cafe A points at Cafe B?
No. Every business has its own balance. Display totals are informational only and never redeemable across shops.
How do I earn?
Merchant enters your bill, shows a one-time QR (5 min expiry). You scan while logged in and confirm. Server validates once — replays rejected.
Do I need SMS OTP?
Email OTP works now. SMS shows an honest “setup required” state until Twilio Verify is configured. We never fake OTP success.
Do maps work?
Manual addresses work today. Google Maps lights up automatically once keys are configured; otherwise a setup notice is shown.
Is feedback mandatory?
No. Optional stars/review after a visit. Skipping never loses rewards.
